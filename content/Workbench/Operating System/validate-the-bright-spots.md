---
title: Validate the bright spots
---
**Principle:**  
Start from what is valid, not what is wrong.

**Insight:**  
Across psychology, parenting, and organizational change, attempts to directly correct problems create resistance. Progress happens faster when you first anchor to something that already is working—emotionally, behaviorally, or systemically, and reinforce that.

**Heuristic:**  
Before changing anything, explicitly validate what is already working or understandable.

**Implication:**  
Correction without validation destabilizes. Validation creates acceptance, which facilitates change.
